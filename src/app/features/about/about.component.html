Our History & Engineering Heritage

Engineering Capabilities Meets Modern Smart Vending

Founded in Cairo in 2010, Infinity Tecs originally focused on high-precision Automation and Machine Control Solutions across critical industrial segments including Water Treatment, Corporate Workspaces, and Food & Beverage (F&B).

Leveraging over a decade of deep engineering capabilities in high-tech machine controls, we expanded our operations into commercial automated vending machine systems — providing Egyptian enterprises with world-class coffee hubs, fresh snack centers, and automated citrus juicers.

Today, Infinity Tecs brings together Italian-made vending machines, local installation and after-sales support, and smart operations tools for businesses across Egypt.
